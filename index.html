About

At Meta, Bess set the content strategy for how 3B+ people introduce themselves on Facebook, led content for a privacy change affecting 100M+ users, and built an AI agent that audits product code for content issues. Today she designs content for TikTok’s advertiser tools.

Before tech, she spent a decade teaching and researching rhetoric: the study of how words persuade. She still uses that training in research, critique, and mentoring.

Off the clock, find her in ballet class, hiking Bay Area trails, or knitting under the watchful eye of her sassy tabby, Ezra.
